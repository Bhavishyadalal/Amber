Good to know

Where are my files saved?

Beside Amber.exe. Give Amber its own folder, such as Desktop\Amber, and your downloads, queue and settings all stay inside it.

Do I need to install anything?

No installer. Amber runs from a single Amber.exe. If the window opens blank, install the Microsoft WebView2 Runtime.

Does the demo download anything?

No. The demo uses sample songs and videos. Nothing is fetched from YouTube or saved to your device. The real app does both.

Is it okay to save any video?

Save only what you have the right to keep. Amber is for personal use, and copying YouTube content may breach YouTube's Terms of Service.
